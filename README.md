One day's world news weighed on a single scale — a giant antique brass balance holding a peace scroll on one side and dark rubble on the other. Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 26 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261126).

## The six stories

- **Shenzhen:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first-ever trilateral summit and adopt a 4-point peace declaration.
- **Saudi Arabia:** Houthi missiles toward Riyadh intercepted (2 missiles); 1 killed at the Jazan refinery.
- **Gaza:** Israeli overnight strikes killed 9, including Hamas-appointed deputy education minister Khaled Abu Nada.
- **Kyiv:** Russian ballistic missiles hit a shopping centre and two other buildings; 7 wounded.
- **Indonesia:** Flooded illegal gold mine in West Java; 16 killed, 10 missing.
- **DR Congo:** Ebola cases pass 12,021, with 5,904 deaths — the second-largest outbreak on record.

Every number in the image was verified against reporting on the day; no unverified figure is used. Figures that sources disagree on are left out.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` — it copies `news.png` (1024×1024) to `/output/entry.png`. Set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to write elsewhere. The program is deterministic: no network, no randomness, no timestamps, so two runs write byte-identical files.
